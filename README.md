### Currently Building

**BCRP Analytics — Nowcasting Lab**

A research project for experimenting with nowcasting models for Peruvian macroeconomic series using data from the Central Reserve Bank of Peru (BCRP)

The project will be evolving into a small research platform to track forecasts , compare predictions with realized values, and monitor model performance over time while I also learn along the way
